5 Reasons To Leave Your Desk At Lunch

June 21, 2024

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

When work gets busy, eating lunch at your desk can feel like the easiest option. You can answer emails, finish a project, and squeeze in a few bites without stepping away from your computer. But while it might seem productive, skipping a proper break can leave you feeling tired, distracted, and less motivated throughout the afternoon.

Taking time away from your desk gives you an opportunity to recharge, enjoy your meal, and return to work with a fresh perspective. Even a short lunch break can make a difference in your daily routine. Leaving your desk at lunch doesn't have to mean taking a full hour away from work. Even 15 to 30 minutes spent eating without distractions, stretching your legs, or enjoying a little fresh air can make your day feel better balanced.

1. Give Your Brain a Break.

Focusing on work for hours without a meaningful break can contribute to mental fatigue. Stepping away from your desk allows you to shift your attention away from deadlines, emails, and notifications. A change of scenery can help you feel refreshed and ready to tackle the rest of your day.

2. Enjoy Your Food More Mindfully.

When you eat while answering emails or attending virtual meetings, it's easy to become distracted and lose track of how much you've eaten. Taking a proper lunch break lets you focus on your meal, appreciate its flavors, and pay attention to your body's hunger and fullness cues.

Whenever possible, put your phone away and enjoy your food without multitasking. You'll give yourself a chance to slow down and make lunch a more satisfying part of your day.

3. Get Moving During the Workday.

Sitting for long periods is common in office environments, but lunch is a convenient opportunity to break up that sedentary time. Walking to a nearby café, taking a short stroll outside, or simply moving to another part of the building can help you incorporate more activity into your day.

You don't need a long workout to benefit from movement. Even a brief walk can help you feel more alert and provide a welcome change from sitting in front of a screen.

4. Reduce Stress and Improve Your Mood.

Work can be demanding, especially when deadlines are approaching or your calendar is packed with meetings. A lunch break gives you time to step back from those pressures and do something enjoyable.

Consider eating with a coworker, listening to your favorite music, or spending a few minutes outdoors. Social interaction and time away from work-related demands can help make your day feel more balanced.

5. Return to Work With a Fresh Perspective.

Sometimes, the best way to work through a problem is to stop thinking about it for a while. A change of scenery can give you the mental space to reset, and you may return to a task with a different perspective. Rather than treating lunch as lost productivity, think of it as an opportunity to recharge. A well-timed break can make the afternoon feel more manageable and help you approach your responsibilities with renewed focus.
