GARLIC & BASIL PASTA WITH CLASSIC MARINARA

August 9, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

Few things are as comforting as a bowl of pasta tossed in a rich, homemade marinara sauce. This garlic and basil pasta combines sweet tomatoes, fragrant garlic, fresh basil, and a drizzle of olive oil for a simple Italian-inspired meal that's full of flavor. It's easy to prepare with everyday ingredients and makes a delicious option for busy weeknights.

The secret to a great marinara is letting the tomatoes simmer long enough to develop their natural sweetness while allowing the garlic and herbs to infuse the sauce. Finish with fresh basil and a sprinkle of Parmesan cheese for a classic dish that tastes like it came straight from an Italian kitchen.

Ingredients
Serves 4

  • 12 ounces spaghetti or your favorite pasta
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar (optional, depending on tomato acidity)
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • 1/4 up freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves, for garnish
Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to the package directions until al dente. Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of pasta water.

2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet or saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and optional red pepper flakes. Cook for 30–60 seconds, stirring frequently, until fragrant but not browned.

3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for another minute. Add the crushed tomatoes, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Stir well to combine.

4. Reduce the heat to low and simmer uncovered for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally. If the tomatoes taste particularly acidic, add the optional sugar. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.

5. Stir the torn basil leaves into the sauce during the final 2 minutes of cooking, allowing their fresh aroma to infuse the tomatoes.

6. Add the drained pasta directly to the marinara sauce and toss until evenly coated. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of reserved pasta water until it reaches your preferred consistency.

7. Divide the pasta among four bowls and finish with freshly grated Parmesan, a drizzle of olive oil, and extra basil leaves. Serve immediately while hot.
