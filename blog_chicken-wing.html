TRIED & TESTED - BEST WINGS RECIPE

February 11, 2024

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

Few things are as satisfying as a plate of crispy chicken wings fresh out of the oven. Whether you're hosting a game-day party, planning a family gathering, or simply craving a delicious snack, homemade wings are always a crowd-pleaser. The secret to great wings is getting that golden, crispy skin while keeping the meat juicy and tender.

You don't need a deep fryer to make restaurant-style wings at home. With a few simple ingredients, the right cooking temperature, and a flavorful sauce, you can make wings that are crispy on the outside and packed with flavor. This tried-and-tested recipe serves 4 uses a savory seasoning blend and a sticky honey-garlic sauce for the perfect balance of sweet, salty, and tangy.

Ingredients

For the chicken wings:

  • 2 pounds chicken wings, split at the joints with tips removed
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder (not baking soda)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

For the honey-garlic sauce:

  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
  • Optional: a pinch of red pepper flakes for heat
Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Pat the chicken wings thoroughly dry with paper towels. Removing excess moisture is essential for achieving crispy skin.

2. In a large bowl, combine the baking powder, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Add the wings and olive oil, then toss until evenly coated. Use aluminum-free baking powder if you prefer to avoid any potential metallic aftertaste.

3. Place the wings on a wire rack set over a foil-lined baking sheet, leaving space between each piece. The rack allows hot air to circulate around the wings, helping them cook evenly.

4. Bake for 45–50 minutes, turning the wings halfway through cooking. Cook until the skin is golden and crisp and the thickest parts reach an internal temperature of at least 165°F (74°C). For extra crispness, allow a few additional minutes in the oven while monitoring closely.

5. While the wings bake, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant. Stir in the honey, soy sauce, ketchup, and apple cider vinegar. Simmer gently for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened, then remove from the heat.

6. Transfer the cooked wings to a large bowl. Pour the honey-garlic sauce over the wings and toss until each piece is evenly coated. For extra crunch, serve the sauce on the side instead.

7. Arrange the wings on a platter and serve while hot. Pair them with celery sticks, carrot sticks, ranch dressing, or blue cheese dip for a classic wing-night spread.
