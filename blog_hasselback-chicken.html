ROASTED HASSELBACK CHICKEN BREAST

March 13, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

Looking for a chicken dinner that’s as impressive as it is delicious? Roasted Hasselback chicken breast features juicy, tender chicken sliced into pockets and filled with flavorful cheese, fresh spinach, and savory seasonings. Roasted until golden and bubbling, this easy recipe is perfect for a weeknight dinner or a special meal without the extra fuss.

Ingredients
Serves 2
  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (optional, for garnish)
Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Lightly grease a baking dish with olive oil or cooking spray.

2. Place each chicken breast on a cutting board. Using a sharp knife, make several crosswise cuts about ¾ inch apart, slicing most of the way through without cutting all the way to the bottom.

3. In a bowl, combine the chopped spinach, mozzarella cheese, sun-dried tomatoes, and minced garlic. Mix until evenly combined.

4. Arrange the chicken breasts in the prepared baking dish. Drizzle with olive oil, then season with Italian seasoning, paprika, salt, and black pepper.

5. Carefully spoon the spinach mixture into each cut, distributing the filling evenly between the slices.

6. Bake for 25–35 minutes, depending on the thickness of the chicken breasts. Check the thickest part with a meat thermometer; the chicken must reach an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

7. Let the chicken rest for about 5 minutes before serving. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and serve warm with roasted vegetables, a crisp salad, or your favorite grain.
