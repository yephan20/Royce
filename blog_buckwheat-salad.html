WARM BUCKWHEAT & BEETROOT SALAD

February 08, 2025

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

Cooked, toasted buckwheat has an earthy, slightly nutty flavor, topped with purple onion, walnut, fresh herbs and accompanied with balsamic vinegar. This dish is especially delightful in winter as it goes so well with the earthy senses of mushrooms and the aroma of parsley and thyme. Light in calories but high in protein, filling and naturally gluten-free too, so it is perfect continue your diet-related New Year’s resolutions.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups roasted buckwheat (also known as kasha)
  • 3 large beetroots
  • 2-3 large mushrooms
  • 2 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/3 teaspoon coarse sea salt
  • black pepper
  • 2 tablespoon hazelnuts, chopped
  • 3 tablespoon olive oil
  • 22 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon thyme, minced
  • fresh parsley, to serve
Instructions

1. Heat up the oven to 200° C / 395° F. Peel beetroots and chop them into a large dice.

2. In a pestle and mortar, make thyme salt by pounding the minced thyme and sea salt until you get a fine powder.

3. Place beetroot chunks in a bowl. Coat in 1 tablespoon of olive oil and 1 tablespoon of balsamic vinegar and season with rosemary salt and pepper. Bake for about 40 minutes, until tender.

4. Carefully check buckwheat for small stones and debris. I advise against rinsing it though as it becomes mushy. Put buckwheat into a small pot with a glass lid and add 3 cups / 720 ml of water, cover with a lid and bring to the boil. Do not salt it until it’s been cooked as salt negatively affects buckwheat’s texture. Once the water comes to the boil, turn the heat down low and cook on a low heat until all the water has been absorbed. To check, tip the pot to see if water is coming out from underneath the buckwheat, but DO NOT LIFT THE LID! Once the water has been fully absorbed, rest the pot for another 10 minutes (with the lid firmly on) so that the buckwheat finishes cooking in its own steam.

5. Heat up the remaining two tablespoons of olive oil in a pan. Add diced garlic and fry, on a low heat, until translucent and fragrant. Add sliced mushrooms and fry until cooked. Season with salt and pepper and a tablespoon of balsamic vinegar. Allow it to cook out. Finally add cooked buckwheat. Mix well and adjust the seasoning.

6. Divide between bowls, top with roasted beetroot chunks, hazelnuts and some fresh parsley.
