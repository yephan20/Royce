PUMPKIN SPICE LATTE WITH PUMPKIN SPICE MARSHMALLOWS

September 19, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

Nothing says fall like a warm pumpkin spice latte! This homemade version combines rich coffee, creamy milk, pumpkin puree, and cozy autumn spices. Top it with fluffy pumpkin spice marshmallows for a sweet seasonal treat that’s perfect for chilly mornings or a relaxing afternoon.

Ingredients

For the Pumpkin Spice Latte (2 servings):

  • 2 cups strong brewed coffee or 2 shots espresso
  • 1 1/2 cups milk of your choice
  • 3 tablespoons pumpkin puree
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup or brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Whipped cream (optional)
  • Ground cinnamon for garnish (optional)

For the Pumpkin Spice Marshmallows:

  • 3 tablespoons unflavored gelatin
  • 1/2 cup cold water, divided
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting
Instructions

Make the Pumpkin Spice Marshmallows

1. Pour 1/4 cup cold water into a mixing bowl and sprinkle the gelatin over it. Let it bloom for about 5–10 minutes.

2. In a saucepan, combine the granulated sugar, corn syrup, salt, and remaining ¼ cup water. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Cook until the syrup reaches 240°F (115°C).

3. With a mixer running on low, carefully pour the hot syrup into the bloomed gelatin. Gradually increase the speed to high and whip for 8–12 minutes, until thick, fluffy, and glossy.

4. Beat in the vanilla extract and pumpkin pie spice.

5. Spread the mixture into a lightly greased, powdered-sugar-dusted pan. Let it set at room temperature for at least 4 hours, or overnight. Dust the top with powdered sugar, cut into squares, and toss the cut sides in more powdered sugar.

Make the Pumpkin Spice Latte

1. In a small saucepan, whisk together the milk, pumpkin puree, maple syrup, pumpkin pie spice, and vanilla extract. Warm over medium-low heat until hot, but do not let it boil.

2. Divide the strong brewed coffee or espresso between two mugs.

3. Pour the warm pumpkin mixture into each mug and whisk or froth until creamy.

4. Finish with whipped cream, a sprinkle of cinnamon, and a few pumpkin spice marshmallows.
