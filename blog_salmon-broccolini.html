PAN ROASTED SALMON WITH BROCCOLINI

November 05, 2024

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

One pan, and right ingredients is all it takes for dinner. You can use fillet, but I choose salmon steak because the bones and skin bring out more favor. The skin also contains more of the same protein and essential omega-3 fatty acids. So, discarding the skin is throwing away additional omega-3 fatty acids that your body cannot make.

Ingredients
  • 2 small salmon steaks
  • 1 pound of broccolini
  • 2 garlic gloves minced
  • 1 shallot minced
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric grounded
  • 2 tablespoon soy sauce, divided
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, divided
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil, divided
  • oil for greasing pan
Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425° F. Grease a large baking sheet with a neutral oil.

2. In a large dish, whisk 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 garlic glove minced, 1 teaspoon turmeric grounded, and 1 tbsp sesame oil. Add salmon and marinate for 15 minutes.

3. Meanwhile, in a large bowl whisk remaining soy sauce, vinegar, minced garlic, minced shallot and oil. Toss with broccolini and arrange on the baking sheet. Arrange salmon on sheet.

4. Roast for 20-25 minutes or until cooked through.
