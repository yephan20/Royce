3 MUST-HAVE FOR A HEALTHY STORE BOUGHT SNACK

July 17, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

Life gets busy, and sometimes there's simply no time to prepare snacks from scratch. Whether you're heading to work, running errands, or looking for something quick after a workout, store-bought snacks can be a convenient option. The trick is knowing what to look for when choosing something that's both satisfying and nutritious. Instead of focusing on trendy labels or claims like "natural" and "low-fat," pay attention to the nutrition facts and ingredient list.

1. Look for Protein

You don't necessarily need a high-protein snack every time, but including protein can help make your snack more filling. Good store-bought options include:

  • Greek yogurt with minimal added sugar
  • Roasted chickpeas
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Cheese sticks
  • Protein bars with a reasonable amount of added sugar

2. Choose Fiber-Rich Ingredients

Fiber supports digestive health and can help you feel satisfied for longer. Many packaged snacks contain refined grains and added sugars but very little fiber, so checking the label can help you find better options.

Look for snacks made with whole grains, fruits, vegetables, beans, nuts, or seeds. Air-popped popcorn, whole-grain crackers, fresh fruit, and roasted edamame are convenient choices.

As a general guide, compare similar products and choose options that provide more fiber per serving. Ingredients such as whole oats, whole-wheat flour, and legumes are good signs that a snack may offer more nutritional value.

3. Keep Added Sugar in Check

A little sweetness can certainly fit into a balanced diet, but some packaged snacks contain more added sugar than you might expect. Granola bars, flavored yogurts, dried fruit snacks, and sweetened beverages are common examples.

When comparing products, check the Added Sugars line on the Nutrition Facts label. The American Heart Association recommends limiting added sugar to no more than 25 grams per day for most women and 36 grams per day for most men, although individual needs vary.

Choose unsweetened or lightly sweetened options when possible, and look for snacks that get their sweetness from whole fruit rather than relying heavily on added sugars.

Make Smarter Snack Choices

Remember, no single snack needs to be perfect. The overall quality of your diet matters more than any individual food, and convenience foods can absolutely have a place in a balanced eating routine. Keep a few nutritious options on hand, read labels when comparing products, and choose snacks that taste good and help you feel satisfied between meals.
