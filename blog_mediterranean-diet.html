THE MEDITERREAN DIET - WHY IT’S THE BEST

January 05, 2024

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

The Mediterranean diet is widely recognized as one of the healthiest eating patterns in the world. Inspired by traditional foods and cooking habits in countries such as Greece, Italy, and Spain, this approach focuses on fresh ingredients, healthy fats, whole grains, and plenty of fruits and vegetables. Rather than following strict rules or eliminating entire food groups, it encourages balance, variety, and enjoying meals.

One reason the Mediterranean diet stands out is that it emphasizes foods that are both nutritious and satisfying. Olive oil, fish, beans, nuts, and whole grains provide important nutrients, while herbs and spices add flavor without relying heavily on salt. Research has also linked this eating pattern to better heart health and a lower risk of several chronic diseases. Best of all, it's flexible enough to fit a variety of tastes and lifestyles.

What Foods Make Up the Mediterranean Diet?
  • Fruits and vegetables: Berries, leafy greens, tomatoes, peppers, and citrus fruits provide vitamins, minerals, and fiber.
  • Whole grains: Oats, brown rice, whole-wheat bread, and barley supply fiber and energy.
  • Healthy fats: Extra-virgin olive oil, nuts, seeds, and avocados provide predominantly unsaturated fats.
  • Fish and seafood: Salmon, sardines, and other seafood offer protein and, in many cases, omega-3 fatty acids.
  • Beans and legumes: Lentils, chickpeas, and beans provide plant-based protein and fiber.
  • Herbs and spices: Garlic, basil, oregano, and rosemary add flavor to meals.
  • Dairy and poultry: Yogurt, cheese, eggs, and poultry can be enjoyed in moderate amounts.
  • Red and processed meat: These are generally eaten less frequently than fish, legumes, and other protein sources.
Why Is the Mediterranean Diet So Healthy?

1. Supports Heart Health

One of the best-known benefits of the Mediterranean diet is its association with improved cardiovascular health. Olive oil, nuts, seeds, and fish provide unsaturated fats that can help improve cholesterol levels when they replace saturated fats in the diet. Fruits, vegetables, and whole grains also contribute fiber and other heart-supporting nutrients.

2. Helps Support Healthy Weight Management

Because the diet includes fiber-rich vegetables, legumes, fruits, and whole grains, meals can be filling and satisfying. Healthy fats and protein also help make meals more enjoyable. Although the Mediterranean diet isn't specifically designed for weight loss, it can support weight management when portions and overall energy intake align with your needs.

3. Promotes Digestive Health

Fiber plays an important role in maintaining regular bowel movements and supporting beneficial gut bacteria. The Mediterranean diet includes plenty of fiber-rich foods, such as beans, lentils, whole grains, fruits, vegetables, nuts, and seeds. Increasing fiber gradually and drinking enough water can help you enjoy these benefits comfortably.

1. Provides a Sustainable Way to Eat

Unlike restrictive diets that require you to avoid many favorite foods, the Mediterranean approach allows flexibility. You can enjoy a variety of ingredients, experiment with new recipes, and adapt meals to your budget and preferences. This flexibility can make it easier to maintain healthy eating habits over time.

How to Start Eating the Mediterranean Way

1. Use olive oil more often. Try extra-virgin olive oil in salad dressings, roasted vegetables, and everyday cooking.

2. Add more vegetables. Include vegetables with lunch and dinner, whether they're fresh, roasted, steamed, or sautéed.

3. Eat fish regularly. Aim to include fish or seafood about twice a week, choosing options you enjoy.

4. Choose whole grains. Swap refined grains for whole-grain bread, brown rice, oats, or quinoa more often.

5. Include beans and nuts. Add chickpeas to salads, lentils to soups, or a small handful of nuts to breakfast.

6. Cook with herbs and spices. Use garlic, lemon, basil, oregano, and other seasonings to build flavor without relying too heavily on salt.
