PROTEIN AND NUTRITION DENSE BROWNIE

June 03, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

Who says brownies can't be both delicious and nutritious? These fudgy chocolate brownies are made with wholesome ingredients, including black beans, almond butter, cocoa powder, and protein-rich Greek yogurt. They deliver a rich chocolate flavor and a soft, moist texture while adding protein, fiber, and other nutrients to your favorite treat.

Ingredients
Makes 9 brownies

  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed thoroughly
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup natural almond butter
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup chocolate or vanilla protein powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup dark chocolate chips
  • Optional: 2 tablespoons chopped walnuts or pecans
Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line an 8-by-8-inch baking pan with parchment paper, leaving a little overhang to make the brownies easier to remove.

2. Add the black beans, eggs, almond butter, cocoa powder, maple syrup, Greek yogurt, protein powder, vanilla extract, baking powder, and salt to a food processor. Blend until the mixture is completely smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.

3. Transfer the batter to a bowl and fold in the dark chocolate chips. If you're using chopped walnuts or pecans, add them now as well.

4. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and spread it evenly with a spatula. Sprinkle a few extra chocolate chips on top if desired.

5. Bake for approximately 20–25 minutes, until the edges are set and the center is just firm. Avoid overbaking, as the brownies will continue to set as they cool.

6. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before cutting them into nine squares. For an even fudgier texture, refrigerate them for about 30 minutes before serving.

7. Enjoy your brownies as a snack, dessert, or post-workout treat alongside a glass of milk or your favorite unsweetened milk alternative.
