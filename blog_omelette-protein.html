LOADED OMELETTE, PACKED WITH PROTEIN

August 12, 2024

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

A loaded omelette is a delicious way to start your day with plenty of protein and flavorful ingredients. Eggs provide high-quality protein and essential nutrients, while vegetables add fiber, vitamins, and minerals. Add a little cheese and your favorite lean protein, and you've got a satisfying breakfast that can keep you going throughout the morning.

The best part about an omelette is how easy it is to customize. Whether you prefer spinach and mushrooms, colorful bell peppers, or diced turkey, you can mix and match ingredients to suit your taste. This protein-packed version combines eggs, lean turkey, vegetables, and cheese for a hearty breakfast that's easy to make at home. This recipe provides approximately 30–35 grams of protein and serves 1.

Ingredients
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup cooked diced turkey breast
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup diced bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup fresh spinach
  • 2 tablespoons diced onion
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil or butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: chopped chives or parsley for garnish
Instructions

1. Dice the turkey, bell pepper, and onion. Slice the mushrooms and roughly chop the spinach.

2. Heat the olive oil or butter in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, bell pepper, and mushrooms. Cook for 3–4 minutes until softened, then stir in the spinach and cook until wilted. Transfer the vegetables to a plate.

3. Crack the eggs into a bowl, season with salt and pepper, and whisk until the yolks and whites are fully combined.

4. Pour the eggs into the same skillet over medium-low heat. Gently tilt the pan to spread the mixture evenly. Cook for 2–3 minutes, lifting the edges occasionally to let uncooked egg flow underneath.

5. Once the eggs are mostly set, add the cooked turkey, sautéed vegetables, and shredded cheddar to one half of the omelette.

6. Carefully fold the omelette in half and cook for another 1–2 minutes, until the cheese melts and the eggs are fully cooked.

Transfer to a plate and garnish with fresh chives or parsley. Serve with whole-grain toast, fresh fruit, or sliced avocado for a balanced breakfast.
