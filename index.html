DATA-DRIVEN WORKOUT

Combining cardio and Pilates creates a balanced, highly effective fitness routine. While cardio builds heart health and burns calories, Pilates enhances core strength, flexibility, and muscle tone. Together, they yield significant results in body fat reduction, endurance, and cardiovascular function.

INJURY PREVENTION

The core stability and pelvic strength gained from Pilates protect your joints (knees and hips) from the high impact of cardio.

ENHANCED PERFORMANCE

Pilates teaches you to use oxygen more effectively through breath control, increasing endurance during high-intensity cardio.

PREVENTS PLATEAUS

Mixing controlled resistance with cardiovascular output introduces muscle confusion, boosting your metabolic rate. Muscle confusion elevates your overall metabolic rate.

ALTERNATE DAYS

Perform cardio one day, and use the following day as an active Pilates recovery day. Royce offers both classes, so you should be able to knock this one out of the park.

A BALANCED LIFESTYLE

Combining regular exercise with a balanced diet is widely recognized as the most effective strategy for managing weight and metabolic health. Research shows that dietary changes drive about 80% of weight loss, while exercise is crucial for preserving lean muscle mass and preventing weight regain.
