HEALTHY HOLIDAY EATING - 5 TIPS TO SAVOR THE SEASON

December 03, 2024

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

The holiday season is a time for gathering with family, celebrating traditions, and enjoying some of our favorite foods. From festive dinners and homemade cookies to seasonal drinks and holiday parties, there are plenty of delicious temptations around every corner. With so much to enjoy, it can be easy to lose track of your usual eating habits.

The good news is that healthy holiday eating doesn't mean giving up your favorite dishes or skipping dessert. Instead, it's about finding a balance that lets you enjoy the season while feeling your best. A few simple habits can help you make mindful choices, avoid feeling overly stuffed, and savor every bite without guilt.

1. Don't Skip Meals to Save Calories.

It might seem like a good idea to skip breakfast or lunch before a big holiday dinner, but arriving at the table overly hungry can make it harder to recognize when you're satisfied. Instead, eat your regular meals and choose balanced options that include protein, fiber, and healthy fats. A light breakfast or a nourishing lunch can help you arrive at dinner feeling comfortable rather than ravenous.

2. Fill Your Plate With Variety.

Holiday meals often feature rich favorites alongside nutritious seasonal options. Take advantage of the variety by including vegetables, fruit, lean proteins, and whole grains on your plate. Roasted Brussels sprouts, sweet potatoes, green beans, and winter squash can add color, flavor, and nutrients to your meal. You can still enjoy traditional favorites—just aim for a balance that leaves you feeling satisfied.

3. Enjoy Your Favorite Treats Mindfully.

Cookies, pies, stuffing, and creamy casseroles are part of what makes holiday meals special. Rather than labeling foods as good or bad, choose the dishes you genuinely enjoy and take time to savor them. Eat slowly, pay attention to the flavors, and give yourself permission to enjoy dessert. Mindful eating can help you appreciate your food and recognize when you've had enough.

4. Stay Hydrated and Be Mindful of Drinks.

Between holiday cocktails, sweetened coffee drinks, and festive punches, beverages can contribute more sugar and calories than you might realize. Make water your go-to drink throughout the day, and consider alternating alcoholic or sugary beverages with water or sparkling water. Unsweetened tea and water infused with citrus or berries are also refreshing options. Staying hydrated is especially helpful when you're busy with holiday activities.

5. Keep Moving and Get Enough Rest.

Healthy habits extend beyond what's on your plate. A family walk after dinner, dancing at a holiday party, or playing outside with the kids can help you stay active while making memories. Sleep matters, too. Aim for a consistent sleep schedule whenever possible, since adequate rest supports overall well-being and helps regulate appetite. Remember, movement should be enjoyable—not a punishment for eating holiday foods.

Make Room for Enjoyment

Healthy holiday eating isn't about perfection. It's about enjoying the foods and traditions you love while making choices that help you feel good. One large holiday meal won't undo your usual habits, just as one nutritious meal won't define your overall health. What matters most is the pattern of choices you make over time.

This season, focus on balance rather than restriction. Share meals with loved ones, enjoy your favorite treats, stay active in ways you find fun, and listen to your body's hunger and fullness cues. With a little planning and a flexible mindset, you can savor the holiday season while keeping your well-being in mind.
