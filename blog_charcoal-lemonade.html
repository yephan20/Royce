CHARCOAL LEMONADE WITH CHIA

July 18, 2025

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

When life hands you lemons, make lemonade! Charcoal lemonade is the perfect way to stay hydrated, detox, and get a taste of spring all in one sip. Activated charcoal is different from regular charcoal or char. The latter are carcinogens, whereas activated charcoal helps with everything from detoxification to digestion to even seriously curing my stomach flu. Activated charcoal is made from everything from bone char to coconuts or olive pits. It’s processed at a very high temperature to “activate” it, changing its structure and giving it those amazing benefits. The best part is perhaps that it really doesn’t taste at all, and the powder is so fine that it blends in easily to most liquids.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup chia seeds
  • 1/2 cup light maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice (about 4 lemons)
  • 3 cups cold water
Instructions

1. Soak the chia seeds in a bowl with some water overnight.

2. In a pitcher, stir together maple syrup, lemon juice, and water. Add more maple syrup for sweetness or lemon juice for tartness.

3. Stir in charcoal powder.

4. Add soaked chia seeds.

5. Pour into glasses over ice.
