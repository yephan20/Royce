UPGRADE YOUR TACO TUESDAY WITH THESE CHICKEN TACOS

November 14, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

Taco Tuesday just got a delicious upgrade! These juicy, flavorful chicken tacos are easy to prepare, loaded with fresh ingredients, and perfect for a quick weeknight dinner. Tender chicken seasoned with smoky spices is paired with crunchy cabbage, creamy avocado, and a zesty lime dressing for a combination that's fresh, satisfying, and full of flavor. Serve them with black beans, Mexican-style rice, or fresh salsa for a complete meal the whole family will love.

Ingredients
Serves 4 (8 tacos)

For the chicken:

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Juice of 1/2 lime

For the tacos:

  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded red or green cabbage
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup finely diced red onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 cup shredded Monterey Jack or cheddar cheese (optional)
  • Lime wedges, for serving

For the creamy lime sauce:

  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • A pinch of salt
  • Optional: a dash of hot sauce
Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Rub the chicken with olive oil, lime juice, and the seasoning mixture until evenly coated.

2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook for approximately 5–7 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until golden and cooked through. The thickest part should reach an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). Transfer to a cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes.

3. While the chicken cooks, whisk together the Greek yogurt, lime juice, honey, salt, and optional hot sauce in a small bowl. Add a teaspoon of water if needed to achieve a drizzle-friendly consistency.

4. Cut the rested chicken into thin strips or bite-sized pieces. Toss with any flavorful juices left on the cutting board.

5. Heat the tortillas in a dry skillet for about 20–30 seconds per side until warm and flexible. Keep them covered with a clean kitchen towel while preparing the tacos.

6. Divide the chicken among the tortillas. Top with shredded cabbage, avocado, tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, and cheese, if desired. Drizzle with the creamy lime sauce.

7. Finish with a squeeze of fresh lime juice and serve immediately while the chicken and tortillas are warm.
