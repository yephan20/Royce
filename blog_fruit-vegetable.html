POWER FRUITS, VEGETABLES & NUTS TO BOOST YOUR WORKOUT

October 23, 2023

By Cat Pedrick-Graybeck

SHARE THIS POST

Whether you're hitting the gym, going for a run, or squeezing in a quick home workout, the foods you eat can make a difference in how you feel and perform. Fruits, vegetables, and nuts provide important nutrients that help fuel physical activity, support muscle recovery, and keep your body functioning at its best. The key is choosing a variety of nutrient-rich foods and eating enough to match your activity level.

Best Fruits to Fuel Your Workout
  • Bananas: A popular pre-workout snack, bananas provide carbohydrates and potassium, which supports normal muscle function. They're easy to digest and convenient to eat on the go.
  • Berries: Blueberries, strawberries, and raspberries contain antioxidants and vitamin C. Add them to yogurt, oatmeal, or a post-workout smoothie.
  • Oranges: Oranges provide carbohydrates, vitamin C, and fluids. Their refreshing taste makes them a great snack before or after physical activity.
  • Tart cherries: Tart cherries contain plant compounds called polyphenols that have been studied for their potential role in exercise recovery and muscle soreness.
  • Watermelon: With its high water content, watermelon can contribute to hydration while providing carbohydrates and beneficial plant compounds.
Vegetables That Support Performance and Recovery
  • Spinach: This leafy green provides folate, vitamin K, and iron, which plays an important role in transporting oxygen throughout the body.
  • Sweet potatoes: Rich in carbohydrates and beta-carotene, sweet potatoes make a satisfying addition to meals before or after a workout.
  • Beets: Beets contain naturally occurring nitrates, which the body can convert into nitric oxide. Research suggests dietary nitrates may improve exercise efficiency for some people.
  • Bell peppers: Packed with vitamin C, bell peppers are easy to add to salads, omelets, stir-fries, and grain bowls.
  • Broccoli: Broccoli provides fiber, vitamin C, and folate, making it a nutritious choice for balanced meals that support an active lifestyle.
Nuts to Add to Your Fitness Routine
  • Almonds: Almonds provide protein, vitamin E, magnesium, and healthy fats. They're an easy snack to pack for busy days.
  • Walnuts: Walnuts are a source of plant-based omega-3 fatty acids, particularly alpha-linolenic acid (ALA), as well as antioxidants.
  • Pistachios: These nuts provide protein, fiber, and potassium, making them a convenient addition to a balanced diet.
  • Cashews: Cashews contain magnesium, copper, and healthy fats. Enjoy them as a snack or blend them into creamy sauces.
  • Peanuts: Technically legumes rather than tree nuts, peanuts provide protein, healthy fats, and niacin. Peanut butter can also be paired with fruit for a satisfying snack.
How to Eat for Better Workouts

1. Before exercise: Try a banana, a piece of toast with peanut butter, or yogurt with berries. Choose foods that sit comfortably in your stomach.

2. After exercise:. Combine protein and carbohydrates, such as Greek yogurt with fruit, eggs with whole-grain toast, or chicken with sweet potatoes and vegetables.

3. Stay hydrated: Drink water regularly throughout the day and replace fluids lost through sweat. Longer or particularly sweaty workouts may require additional electrolytes.

4. Keep snacks simple: Pack fruit and a small portion of nuts so you have a convenient option when hunger strikes.
